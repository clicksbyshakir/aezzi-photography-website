Behind the lens

Abdulhussain Ezzi

Photographer · Videographer · Content creator — Nairobi, Kenya

Hello! I’m Abdulhussain Ezzi, a Nairobi-based photographer with a passion to freeze moments of life in time, leaving a digital footprint in this forever-changing world.

My photography journey began with a point-and-shoot camera while photographing (the late) Sudan, the last male northern white rhino, at Ol Pejeta Conservancy — a pivotal moment that ignited my love for this art form.

I’m dedicated to telling the stories of the voiceless, especially through wildlife photography. I also bring a unique perspective to interior and architecture photography: with an artistic vision and keen attention to minute details, I capture the unique angles and details that others might overlook.

With a background in graphic design, videography, social media content creation and 2D/3D animation, I bring a distinctive creative vision to the table in each of my projects.

Outside of photography, I’m an avid fan of F1, football, safaris and travel. I also enjoy exploring astrophotography, fireworks photography and sports photography.

Thank you for visiting my site. I’m excited to share my visual stories with you!
